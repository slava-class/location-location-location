  Features:

    - Add a shortcut-toggleable live controls panel that stays open while inspecting the factory.
    - Show hovered inserter and ghost transfer capacity, with explicit estimates and unsupported cases.
    - Add independent inserter, mining-drill and fluid-connection diagnostics with local ground hand-off detection.
  Compatibility:
    - Require Inserter Throughput Lib 2.1.0 or newer.
    - Replace Inserter Throughput, its fork and Fluid Connection Indicators; simultaneous activation is rejected.
  Bugfixes:
    - Place splitter filter icons on their configured output through rotation and priority changes.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Features:
    - Independently authored contextual hover information and nearby recipe-only presentation.
    - Quiet joint native fluid decluttering with per-player local background and construction detail.
    - Independent optional resource icons, electrical bars, ammunition counts and temporary native ALT.
    - Per-player presentation ownership, selection cleanup and English/German settings text.
  Compatibility:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1.21 or newer; no required runtime library.
    - Explicitly excludes hover-alt-mode, Cleaner-Alt-Mode and alt-alt-mode.
    - Keeps recipe-name text, prototype storage layouts and diagnostic mods separate.
  Notes:
    - The initial nearby recipe radius is a calibration trial, not a measured performance recommendation.
    - Startup fluid suppression is shared by all players; local presentation preferences do not restore native prototypes.
