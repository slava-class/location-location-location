### Short summary

Plan where your next production line belongs. Choose a recipe, survey its ingredient sources, and save a suggested or hand-picked site as a shared map tag.

## Description

**Find a place for your next production line.**

You have a recipe in mind, but its ingredients are scattered across the factory. Location Location Location helps you survey those sources and save a production-site plan before laying belts, pipes, or rails.

### Make your first plan

1. Open the planner with **Control-Shift-L** (default), its shortcut, or its top-left button.
2. Click **+** and choose a product and recipe. Search by name, show unresearched recipes, or **Copy from machine** to read one configured assembler, furnace, or silo.
3. Assign sources with **Select** or **Add closest**. Pin the editor to keep the world interactive while surveying.
4. Review the suggested site, or use **Choose marker location** and drag an area to place the marker at its center.
5. Click **Apply** to save the recipe tag for your force.

You can save a recipe without sources. It stays in the list; a map marker appears once you assign sources or choose a marker position.

### Features

- Add the closest configured producer or find it with a temporary navigation pin.
- Suggest a site from ingredient source centers, or choose the marker position yourself.
- Pin the editor, highlight sources, and browse plans across available surfaces.
- Keep drafts private until Apply; reopen, retire, or delete shared plans.

This is a geometric planning aid; it does not calculate throughput or build your factory.

### Requirements

Requires **Factorio 2.1.20 or later within 2.1** and **Factorio Library 0.17.2 or later**. Space Age is optional. The interface includes English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, and Simplified Chinese.
