Version: 3.3.0
Date: 26 September 2026
  Features:
    - Add surface-based navigation for saved plans, using actual planets, space platforms, and modded surfaces rather than logical districts.
    - Keep the plan list beside a two-panel ingredient editor with native Factory Planner-style framing, compact controls, and a suggested-location preview.
  Changes:
    - Keep product and recipe selection in a fixed-size modal over the edited plan, with stable search and footer positions across filtering and empty results.
    - Show unapplied draft changes beside Apply while preserving saved assignment counts in the sidebar.
    - Use denser ingredient rows with explicit source counts and missing-source states; rename Select closest to Add closest.
    - Separate immediate Retire/Delete controls from the Cancel/Apply footer, align plan counts, and distinguish retired rows.
    - Align recipe controls and label the preview's actual suggested position.
    - Share caption, button, icon, subheader, window, and footer construction between planner and picker screens.
    - Group plan lifecycle actions separately from rail navigation, use one full-width hit target per plan, and match the empty recipe selector to the light-green Add button.
    - Keep panel headers flush while insetting their contents; give visible feedback a distinct surface and return its space to the list when dismissed.
    - Remove empty recipe-choice slots and redundant product-grid framing, label recipe groups, and group consistently sized picker back actions together.
    - Use Factorio's built-in recessed-slot background without an extra surrounding frame or new image assets; align its visible border with the product tabs.
    - Make titlebar toggles smaller with more space between them and less before Close; reduce the empty-recipe plus, tighten plan rows, and preserve ingredient-action clearance beside scrollbars.
    - Browsing another surface discards private edits without moving the player, saved sources, or markers; new plans are created only on the current surface.
  Bugfixes:
    - Update active-plan source highlights immediately when editing source assignments, including Clear and right-drag removal.
    - Preserve shared sources used by other active plans, restore saved highlights on Cancel, and keep draft previews private until Apply.
    - Show a white modal highlight icon when inactive and a black icon when active.
    - Show source lookup feedback in a dismissible status bar without moving the list's top edge or dialog footer; clear stale feedback on retries or source edits.
    - Keep the full Selecting label visible.
